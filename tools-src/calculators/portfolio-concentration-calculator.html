About the math: each weight is the holding value divided by the portfolio total. The HHI score is the sum of the squared weights in percent, so a 50% holding contributes 2,500. Under 1,500 reads as diversified, 1,500 to 2,500 as moderate, and over 2,500 as concentrated.
