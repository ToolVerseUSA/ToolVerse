About the math: contribution margin is price minus variable cost per unit. Break-even units are fixed costs plus your profit target, divided by contribution margin. Break-even revenue is break-even units times price. Profit at any level is units times contribution margin, minus fixed costs.

Worked example: with $10,000 fixed costs per month, a $50 price, and a $30 variable cost, the margin is $20, break-even is 500 units, and break-even revenue is $25,000 per month. With a $2,000 profit target, break-even rises to 600 units and $30,000 revenue.

Note: this is the general business break-even. ToolVerse also has a mortgage refinance break-even calculator and a relocation break-even calculator for those specific uses.
