How to improve each factor

  • Payment history: Pay every bill on time — set up autopay for at least the minimum payment.
  • Utilization: Pay card balances down below 30% of your limits, and under 10% if you can.
  • Length of history: Keep your oldest card open, even if you rarely use it — closing it shortens your history.
  • New credit: Apply for new credit only when you need it — each hard inquiry can lower your score for a while.
  • Credit mix: Do not open accounts just for variety — a mix helps a little, but on-time payments and low balances matter far more.
