Federal income tax is separate

Self-employment tax is only one part of what you may owe. Federal income tax is separate, and it depends on your total income from all sources and your filing status. It is not included in the estimate above.

For tax year 2026, the standard deduction is $16,100 for single filers and $32,200 for married couples filing jointly, and the federal marginal rates are 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, and 37%. The 37% rate starts above $640,600 of taxable income for single filers and above $768,700 for married couples filing jointly. Source: IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32, announced in IR-2025-103. Figures for 2025 differ — check the IRS for that year.
