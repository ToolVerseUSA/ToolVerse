How much do you need to retire?

This calculator uses the 25x rule: your nest egg should be about 25 times what you plan to spend each year in retirement. That comes from the 4% rule — withdrawing 4% of your savings in the first year of retirement has historically lasted about 30 years. Enter your numbers to see your target, your projected balance, and the monthly saving needed to close any gap.
