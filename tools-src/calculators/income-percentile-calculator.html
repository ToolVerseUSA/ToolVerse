How to read this

The median individual income falls in the $37,500 to $39,999 band.

About the data: the 44 bands come from the U.S. Census Bureau, Current Population Survey, 2025 Annual Social and Economic Supplement (CPS ASEC), Table PINC-11, income year 2024, people age 15 and over, including people with no income. Counts are survey estimates, not exact counts. The result is an estimate based on grouped bands, not an exact percentile.
