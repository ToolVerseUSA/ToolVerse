Why this decision needs more than one number

Renting and buying include different upfront costs, recurring costs, flexibility, risks, and time horizons. A comparison should show what has been assumed rather than convert uncertain market outcomes into promises.

Methodology and limitations

ToolVerse totals the rent and renter-insurance path using your annual rent-increase assumption. It totals the down payment, entered closing costs, and monthly ownership costs using your ownership-cost increase assumption. It also shows mortgage principal paid but does not estimate resale value or investment returns.

Questions people ask

Does this tell me whether buying is better than renting?

No. It compares entered cash flows. Flexibility, maintenance responsibility, future plans, local market conditions, property value changes, and personal preferences still matter.

Why does the calculator not predict home appreciation?

Future home values and sale timing are uncertain. Leaving them out makes clear that this result is a cash-flow comparison, not an investment return forecast.

What ownership costs should I include?

The tool includes the property tax, insurance, HOA or condo dues, maintenance reserve, and mortgage-insurance amounts you enter in addition to principal and interest.
