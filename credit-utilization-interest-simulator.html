Frequently asked questions

Will reaching 30% guarantee a higher credit score?

No. Scoring models are proprietary and utilization is only one factor.

How is utilization calculated?

Total reported balances divided by total credit limits, multiplied by 100.

Why can statement timing matter?

Issuers may report balances based on statement cycles, so the balance shown to a scoring model can differ from today’s balance.
