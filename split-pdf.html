How to Split PDF Pages Online for Free

Extracting specific pages or separating a large PDF document into individual files shouldn't be a hassle. With the ToolVerse Split PDF tool, you can instantly divide your documents into manageable parts or extract the exact pages you need directly in your web browser.

Whether you need to pull a single invoice from a monthly report or break down a massive ebook into chapter-by-chapter PDF files, our secure, client-side PDF splitter gets the job done in seconds without the need for premium software or paid subscriptions.

Why Choose Our PDF Splitter?

  • 100% Secure & Private: Your PDF files are processed entirely on your local device. We never upload, store, or view your sensitive data.
  • Custom Page Ranges: Easily specify custom page ranges (e.g., 1-5, 8, 11-13) to extract only the information that matters to you.
  • Extract All Pages as ZIP: Need every page separated? Use our "Extract All" mode to instantly split a PDF into individual files and download them together in a neat ZIP archive.
  • Lightning Fast: Utilizing advanced browser technology, large PDF files are processed instantly without waiting for server uploads or downloads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to split confidential PDF files here?
Yes! Our Split PDF tool is completely secure. It processes your files locally using your browser's processing power. Your files are never uploaded to any external servers, ensuring absolute privacy.
How do I extract specific pages from a PDF?
Simply upload your PDF, select the "Custom Range" mode, and type the page numbers you wish to extract (for example, "1-3, 5, 8-10"). The tool will generate a new PDF containing only those specific pages.
Can I split a PDF into individual single-page files?
Absolutely. Just select the "Extract All" mode after uploading your document. The tool will separate every single page into its own PDF file and package them all into a convenient ZIP folder for you to download in one click.
Are there any file size limits for splitting PDFs?
Because the extraction happens directly on your device rather than on a cloud server, there are no strict file size limits imposed by us. You can process large documents effortlessly as long as your device has enough memory.
