What does the score measure?

It is an educational estimate based on rent, debt, estimated take-home pay, and the selected state's simplified tax rate. It is not financial advice or a lending decision.

How accurate are the tax estimates?

The calculator uses simplified average rates. Actual paychecks vary with filing status, deductions, credits, benefits, local taxes, and other payroll factors.

Are my inputs private?

Calculations run locally in your browser. Recent calculations may be saved in Local Storage and are not sent to a ToolVerse server.
