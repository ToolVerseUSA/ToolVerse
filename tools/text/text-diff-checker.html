When NOT to use Text Diff Checker

  • It compares lines, not words — a one-word change marks the whole line as removed and re-added, so it can't highlight the exact word that changed.
  • Very large documents (thousands of lines on both sides) are refused with a message, because an exact line diff grows quickly in the browser — split the document and compare it in parts.

Questions people ask

Is my text sent to a server?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.

How does the comparison work?

It compares whole lines using a longest-common-subsequence match: lines only in the first text are marked removed, lines only in the second are marked added, and matching lines are unchanged.

Why is a moved paragraph shown as a removal and an addition?

Line diffs match content, not position — a paragraph that moved appears as removed from its old place and added in its new one.
