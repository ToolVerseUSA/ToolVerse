Questions people ask

How long should my title and description be?

Google typically shows around 50-60 characters of the title and about 150-160 of the description, but it varies by device and pixel width. This tool flags when you go over common limits.

Is my content sent anywhere?

No. Everything renders locally in your browser from the text you type. Nothing is uploaded.

How accurate is the preview?

It is an approximation using common character limits. Google may rewrite titles and descriptions, so treat this as a planning preview, not a guarantee.
