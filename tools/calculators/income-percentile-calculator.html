How to read this

The median individual income falls in the $37,500 to $39,999 band.

About the data: the 44 bands come from the U.S. Census Bureau, Current Population Survey, 2025 Annual Social and Economic Supplement (CPS ASEC), Table PINC-11, income year 2024, people age 15 and over, including people with no income. Counts are survey estimates, not exact counts. The result is an estimate based on grouped bands, not an exact percentile.

When NOT to use US Income Percentile Calculator

  • This tool compares individual income only. It does not measure household income, wealth, cost of living, or what an income feels like in your city.
  • The result is a band-based estimate from grouped Census data, not an exact percentile, and survey estimates carry sampling error.
  • It uses 2024 income data. Incomes and prices change over time, so newer years will differ.

Questions people ask

Where does this income data come from?

The U.S. Census Bureau, Current Population Survey, 2025 Annual Social and Economic Supplement (CPS ASEC), Table PINC-11, income year 2024. The table covers people age 15 and over, all races, males and females combined, 278,302,000 people in 44 income bands. The published table is at census.gov, and the verified table is saved with this tool.

Is this my exact percentile?

No. Census publishes grouped bands, not a percentile for every dollar amount. This tool finds your band and estimates your position by interpolating evenly inside that band, so treat the number as an approximation, not an exact rank.

Is this household income?

No. This is individual money income before tax, for one person. Household income combines everyone under one roof and is usually higher. The optional household size field is only a note, it does not change the comparison.

What is the median individual income?

The median falls in the $37,500 to $39,999 band. About half of US people age 15 and over earn less than that band and about half earn more, including people with no income.
