What will Medicare cost you each month?

Medicare Part B costs $202.90 per month in 2026, confirmed by CMS on November 14, 2025, with a $283 annual deductible. Higher incomes pay an IRMAA surcharge on top of Part B, based on your modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) from two years earlier. The 2027 Part B premium is not announced yet — the 2026 Trustees Report projects $209.50, shown here as a projection only.

When NOT to use Medicare Cost Estimator

  • Premiums change every year — this estimate uses the confirmed 2026 figures from CMS and a 2027 Part B projection, so re-check before you enroll or budget for a new year.
  • The 2027 IRMAA tiers are not announced yet; this tool shows the 2026 tiers as a guide for 2027, not as 2027 figures.
  • This covers premiums only. Deductibles, copays, coinsurance, Part D drug costs and late-enrollment penalties are not included, and IRMAA on Part D is not added here.

Questions people ask

What is IRMAA?

IRMAA is the Income-Related Monthly Adjustment Amount — an extra charge on your Part B (and Part D) premium when your modified adjusted gross income from two years earlier is above $109,000 (individual) or $218,000 (joint) for 2026. In 2026 the Part B IRMAA runs from $81.20 to $487.00 per month on top of the $202.90 standard premium.

Why do some people pay nothing for Medicare Part A?

Part A is premium-free if you (or your spouse) paid Medicare taxes for at least 40 quarters (10 years). With 30 to 39 quarters the 2026 premium is $311 per month, and with fewer than 30 quarters it is $565 per month.

Is the 2027 Part B premium of $209.50 confirmed?

The 2026 Part B premium is confirmed by CMS at $202.90 per month. The 2027 premium has not been announced yet. The 2026 Trustees Report projects $209.50 for 2027 — this tool labels that figure as a projection only, and uses the 2026 IRMAA tiers as a guide for 2027 because the 2027 tiers are not announced yet.

Can IRMAA also add to my Part D premium?

Yes. IRMAA can also add to your Part D prescription drug plan premium, on top of the plan premium you enter here. This tool does not add a Part D IRMAA amount — check your Part D surcharge at medicare.gov.
