When NOT to use 401(k) Contribution Calculator

  • This is an estimate, not tax or investment advice — IRS limits change every year and your plan may have its own rules.
  • The projection assumes a steady annual return and monthly compounding; real markets go up and down and returns are never guaranteed.
  • It does not include taxes on withdrawals, fees, loans, or Roth vs. traditional differences — check those with your plan documents or a tax professional.

Questions people ask

What is the 401(k) contribution limit for 2026?

For 2026, confirmed by the IRS in Notice 2025-67, the employee elective deferral limit is $24,500. If you are 50 or older you can add a $8,000 catch-up contribution, and if you are 60 to 63 the super catch-up is $11,250. For 2027 the limits are projected at $25,500, $8,500 and $11,750, but the IRS has not confirmed them yet — this tool labels them as a projection.

What is the 401(k) catch-up contribution?

If you are 50 or older you can contribute extra beyond the standard limit. In 2026 that is $8,000 extra. Workers aged 60 to 63 get a higher super catch-up of $11,250 in 2026 under the SECURE 2.0 rules. This tool adds the right catch-up automatically when you enter your age.

How does an employer 401(k) match work?

Many employers match part of what you contribute — for example, 50% of the first 6% of your salary. If you contribute less than the match cap, you leave free money on the table. Enter your employer's match percent and cap above to see exactly how much match you earn.

What happens if I contribute more than the IRS limit?

Your plan will not accept more than the IRS limit for the year, and any excess is usually returned to you. This calculator caps your yearly contribution at the IRS limit for the year you select and shows how much headroom you have left.
