When NOT to use Currency Converter

  • Rates are reference rates for planning — banks and money-transfer services add their own spread and fees, so the amount you actually receive will differ.
  • Never treat a fallback (possibly outdated) rate as a live quote for a real payment or trade — check a live source first when the warning is shown.

Questions people ask

Where do the exchange rates come from?

Live rates come from the Frankfurter API (European Central Bank reference rates), fetched fresh each time you change a currency. If the rate service cannot be reached, the tool falls back to last-known rates recorded 2026-11-03 and clearly labels them as possibly outdated.

Which currencies are supported?

Yes — USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, INR, PKR, CAD, AUD, CHF, CNY, AED, SAR, HKD, SGD, NZD, SEK, NOK, DKK, MXN, BRL, ZAR, KRW, TRY and THB. Rates for PKR, AED and SAR are not published by the Frankfurter source, so those use last-known rates recorded 2026-11-03 and are always labelled as approximate.

Is my amount sent to a server?

No. The conversion math runs entirely in your browser. Only the rate lookup contacts the public rates API, and it sends no personal data — just the currency codes.
