What is DSCR? DSCR is annual net operating income divided by annual debt payments, and lenders usually want at least 1.25, meaning income covers payments 1.25 times over.

About the math: max annual debt service is net operating income divided by DSCR. Max monthly payment is that divided by 12. Max loan amount is the present value of that monthly payment over the term at the monthly rate: PV equals PMT times (1 minus (1 plus i) to the power of minus n) divided by i, where i is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. If the rate is 0, the max loan is the monthly payment times the number of months.

Worked example: with $120,000 net operating income, a DSCR of 1.25, an 8 percent rate, and a 10 year term, max annual debt service is $96,000, max monthly payment is $8,000, and the max loan is about $659,372.

When NOT to use Business Debt Capacity Calculator

  • It uses the DSCR rule alone. Lenders also weigh credit, collateral, and cash flow history, so an approval can be lower.
  • It assumes a fixed interest rate and equal monthly payments for the whole term.
  • It does not include fees, taxes, or other debts the business already carries.

Questions people ask

What is DSCR?

DSCR is annual net operating income divided by annual debt payments. It shows how many times over your income covers your loan payments. Lenders usually want at least 1.25, meaning income covers payments 1.25 times over.

How is the maximum loan worked out?

Max annual debt service is net operating income divided by DSCR. Max monthly payment is that divided by 12. Max loan amount is the present value of that monthly payment over the term at the monthly interest rate.

Can I use EBITDA instead of net operating income?

Yes. Net operating income is often approximated with EBITDA for this kind of estimate, but lenders use their own definitions and may adjust income for owner pay, one-off items, and other factors, so their figure can differ from yours.

Will a lender approve this exact amount?

No. This is an estimate based on the DSCR rule alone. Lenders also check credit, collateral, cash flow history, and other factors, and the loan they offer can be lower than this maximum.

Sources

The DSCR definition and the 1.25 lender convention are the standard lending practice supplied with this build, checked November 2026. The loan math is the standard present value of an annuity formula, and all figures come from the numbers you enter.
