About the math: each weight is the holding value divided by the portfolio total. The HHI score is the sum of the squared weights in percent, so a 50% holding contributes 2,500. Under 1,500 reads as diversified, 1,500 to 2,500 as moderate, and over 2,500 as concentrated.

When NOT to use Portfolio Concentration Calculator

  • This tool measures concentration only. It does not judge quality, value, or future returns.
  • The fall scenarios move one holding only. Real markets move holdings together.
  • The result is a planning measure, not investment advice.

Questions people ask

What is the HHI concentration score?

The Herfindahl-Hirschman Index for a portfolio: add up the square of each holding's weight in percent, so a holding at 50% contributes 2,500. The score runs from near 0 to 10,000. Under 1,500 reads as diversified, 1,500 to 2,500 as moderate, and over 2,500 as concentrated.

What does the largest holding fall show?

How much the whole portfolio would fall if only the largest holding dropped, by 20% and by 50%. It is the largest weight times the fall, so a 50% holding falling 50% cuts the portfolio by 25%.

How many holdings can I enter?

Up to 6. Enter at least 2 holdings with values above zero. Leave the rest blank.

Is a concentrated portfolio bad?

Not always, but it is riskier. One holding can drive the whole result, good or bad. This tool only measures concentration, it does not tell you what to buy or sell.
