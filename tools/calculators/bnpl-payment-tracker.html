      Questions people ask

      What is buy now, pay later?

      Buy now, pay later (BNPL) splits a purchase into small installments — usually 4 payments every 2 weeks (Klarna, Afterpay, Zip) or monthly installments (Affirm). This tracker puts all your BNPL loans in one calendar so no payment sneaks up on you.

      What happens if I miss a BNPL payment?

      It depends on the provider. Some charge late fees, some pause your account, and some report missed payments to credit bureaus. Check your agreement with each provider — the tracker flags payments that land close together so you can plan ahead.

      Does BNPL affect my credit score?

      Some BNPL loans are reported to credit bureaus and some are not — it varies by provider and by loan. Paying on time is the safe rule either way.

      How do I pay off BNPL fastest?

      List every loan with its next due date. The calendar shows the soonest payment first — pay that one first, then work down the list. If two payments land in the same week, the tracker warns you.
