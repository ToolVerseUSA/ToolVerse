When NOT to use Business Loan Payment Calculator

  • This tool assumes a fixed interest rate for the whole term. Variable-rate loans will differ.
  • It covers principal and interest only, not fees, taxes, insurance, or lender charges.
  • The result is an estimate for planning, not a loan offer or a promise of approval.

Questions people ask

How is the monthly payment calculated?

With the standard fixed-rate amortization formula: monthly payment equals principal times the monthly rate times (1 plus monthly rate) to the power of the number of payments, divided by ((1 plus monthly rate) to the power of the number of payments minus 1). The rate is the annual rate divided by 12.

How does an extra monthly payment help?

Every extra dollar goes straight to principal, so the balance falls faster, less interest builds up, and the loan ends sooner. This tool shows the payoff time and total interest with and without the extra payment.

Does this include fees or taxes?

No. This is a principal and interest estimate only. Origination fees, closing costs, taxes, and variable rates are not included, so check the lender's terms before relying on the numbers.

Is this a loan offer?

No. This tool does not offer loans, check credit, or connect to any lender. It only does the math on the numbers you enter.
