About the math: annual salary divided by hours per week times weeks per year gives the hourly rate, and the same formula in reverse gives the salary. A standard full time year is 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, or 2,080 hours. Overtime, taxes, benefits, bonuses, and tips are not included.

When NOT to use Salary to Hourly Calculator

  • It converts gross pay only. It does not subtract taxes, benefits, or retirement contributions.
  • It assumes the same hours every week. Overtime, variable schedules, and unpaid time off need separate math.
  • It does not include bonuses, commissions, or tips.

Questions people ask

How do you convert salary to hourly?

Divide the annual salary by the total hours worked in a year: hours per week times weeks per year. A $52,000 salary at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is $52,000 divided by 2,080 hours, or $25.00 an hour.

How do you convert hourly pay to an annual salary?

Multiply the hourly rate by hours per week, then by weeks per year. At $25.00 an hour, 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, the annual salary is $52,000.

Should I use 52 weeks or fewer?

Use 52 weeks if you are paid for the full year, including paid time off. If you take unpaid time off, use fewer weeks. For example, 50 weeks at 40 hours is 2,000 hours a year.

Does this include taxes or benefits?

No. This converts gross pay only. Taxes, retirement contributions, health insurance, and overtime are not included, so your take home pay will be lower.
