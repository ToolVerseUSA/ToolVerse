When NOT to use Contract Templates

  • Don't sign a contract generated here without reading it fully and, for anything important, having a licensed attorney review it for your state and situation.
  • If the deal involves real estate, employment, intellectual property assignment or large sums, use an attorney-drafted agreement instead of a general template.

Questions people ask

Is my contract data sent to a server?

No. The contract templates run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The names and terms you enter are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Are these templates legally binding?

They are starting-point templates in plain English. They are not legal advice and may not cover your situation or your state's rules. Have a licensed attorney review any contract before you sign it.

How do I download my contract as a PDF?

Use the Print / Download as PDF button. Your browser's print dialog lets you save the contract preview as a PDF file — choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
