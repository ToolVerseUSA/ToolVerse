When NOT to use Invoice Generator

  • Don't use this as your only record for tax purposes — keep a copy of every invoice you send, and check your local tax rules for what an invoice must include.
  • If your business needs recurring invoices, client tracking or payment links, a dedicated invoicing app will save you time over time.

Questions people ask

Is my invoice data sent to a server?

No. The invoice generator runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your seller, buyer and pricing details are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Can I add more than one line item?

Yes. Add as many line items as you need with the Add line button, and remove any line with its Remove button. At least one line is always kept.

How do I download my invoice as a PDF?

Use the Print / Download as PDF button. Your browser's print dialog lets you save the invoice preview as a PDF file — choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.
