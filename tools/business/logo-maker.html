Questions people ask

Is my logo data sent to a server?

No. The logo maker runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript and SVG. Your business name and design choices are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Can I download my logo as a PNG and as an SVG?

Yes. Download PNG gives you a raster image (1200 × 800) for websites and social profiles; Download SVG gives you a vector file you can scale to any size without losing quality.

Is this a full brand identity tool?

This tool creates a simple wordmark-style logo from shapes, colours and text. For a fully custom illustrated mark or a registered trademark, work with a professional designer.
