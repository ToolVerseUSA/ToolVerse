Questions people ask

Are these numbers more likely to win?

No — and beware any tool that claims otherwise. Every combination has exactly the same odds (the Powerball jackpot is 1 in 292,201,338). These are random quick picks, generated the same way a lottery terminal generates them.

Which formats are built in?

Powerball: 5 numbers from 1–69 plus a Powerball from 1–26. Mega Millions (current rules since April 2025): 5 numbers from 1–70 plus a Mega Ball from 1–24. A custom mode covers any other game.

Does the tool sell tickets or check results?

No. It only generates numbers. Buy tickets from an official retailer and check results on your state lottery's official site.
