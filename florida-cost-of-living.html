Cost of Living in Florida (2026)

Florida pairs no state income tax with a cost of living near the national average, but rent is the catch: the average apartment costs $1,995 a month, and Miami runs far above the state average. Here is what that means in real dollars: what rent costs, what salary you need, and what a minimum wage paycheck actually covers.

Can you afford Florida on minimum wage?

At $15.00 an hour, full time work (40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year) pays $31,200 a year, or about $2,600 a month before tax.

The average Florida apartment costs $1,995 a month. That is 77% of a full time minimum wage paycheck. On the 30% rule, a minimum wage worker can afford about $780 a month in rent.

To afford the average Florida rent, you need about $38.37 an hour full time, or about 2.6 minimum wage jobs.

By comparison, the median Florida household earns $77,735 a year. On the 30% rule that covers about $1,943 a month in rent, which is below the $1,995 average. Even the median household cannot afford the average Florida rent on the 30% rule.

Florida taxes

Florida has no state income tax, one of nine states with no tax on wages. That helps take-home pay, but it does not make Florida cheap: the state sales tax is 6%, plus local surtaxes, and homeowners pay property tax set by the county. A primary home gets a homestead exemption of up to $50,000 against most property taxes. Buying: the median Florida single-family home sold for $415,000 in September 2026 (Florida Realtors). If you buy, get a homeowners insurance quote early: Florida premiums vary widely by county and storm exposure.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum wage in Florida in 2026?
$15.00 an hour, effective September 30, 2026, the final step of the Amendment 2 schedule. It was $14.00 through September 29, 2026. Tipped workers earn $11.98 an hour plus tips. There is no change in 2027, and the rate is indexed to inflation from January 1, 2028.
What salary do you need to live in Florida?
To afford the average Florida rent of $1,995 a month on the 30% rule, you need about $79,800 a year, or $38.37 an hour full time. In Miami, the median rent of $1,927 needs about $77,080 a year.
Does Florida have a state income tax?
No. Florida is one of nine states with no tax on wages. The state sales tax is 6%, plus local surtaxes, and homeowners pay county property tax, with a homestead exemption of up to $50,000 on a primary home.
Is Florida expensive compared with the US average?
Florida sits just above the national average. The C2ER cost of living index puts Florida at 100.8 for Q1 2026, about 1% above average and 32nd of 51 states and districts. Housing drives the difference, and Miami runs far above the state average.
What is the median household income in Florida?
$77,735 a year, from the Census Bureau American Community Survey 2024, 1-year estimates. That is about $6,478 a month before tax.
