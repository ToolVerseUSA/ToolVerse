Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS-friendly resume?
ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software used by employers to scan resumes. An ATS-friendly resume is properly formatted and contains specific keywords so the software can easily read and rank your application.
Will this write my entire resume for me?
Yes! This AI tool takes your rough notes and transforms them into a highly professional resume draft, including a summary, core competencies, and high-impact work experience bullet points.
