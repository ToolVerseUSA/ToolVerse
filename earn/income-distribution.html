How individual income is distributed across the United States, from the US Census Bureau. Use this table with the US Income Percentile Calculator to see which band an income falls in.

What this table shows. People 15 years and over, by annual money income, from the Current Population Survey Annual Social and Economic Supplement (CPS ASEC), table PINC-11, income year 2024, published March 2025. Total: 278,302,000 people. Median band: $37,500 to $39,999.

Source and method

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Current Population Survey, 2025 Annual Social and Economic Supplement (CPS ASEC), Table PINC-11, Income Distribution to $250,000 or More for Males and Females: 2024. View the table on census.gov.

Counts combine males and females, all races, people 15 years and over. Shares are each band's people divided by the total of 278,302,000. Cumulative share is the running total from the lowest band upward. Figures are rounded to two decimals, so shares may not add to exactly 100 percent.
