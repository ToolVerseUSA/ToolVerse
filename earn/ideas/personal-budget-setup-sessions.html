Personal Budget Setup Sessions

Business Experiment, Unvalidated

Category: Skills offer

A one-hour session that turns a person's pay, bills, and debts into a simple written budget they can follow without a new app.

Who might buy it

People who earn a steady income but feel unsure where the money goes each month. This is a customer hypothesis to test, not evidence of existing demand.

Your first experiment

Run one free session with someone you know, build their budget on a single page, and check in two weeks later to see whether they still use it.

How you could deliver it

Meet by video call or in person, then send the one-page budget and a short checklist of the next three steps.

What you bring

Basic budgeting knowledge, patience with personal numbers, and time to prepare and follow up after each session.

BudgetingPersonal financeCoaching

What would make it worth continuing?

Keep going if clients still follow the budget after two weeks and can name one change it helped them make, since that is stronger evidence than a polite thank you.

What could go wrong?

The risk is that people enjoy the session but drift back to old habits, or that they expect ongoing support and reminders that one session was never meant to include.
