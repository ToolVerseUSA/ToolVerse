Frequently asked questions

What counts as an essential expense?

Expenses needed to maintain basic housing, food, utilities, transportation, insurance, and required obligations are common starting points.

How is runway calculated?

Liquid savings divided by essential monthly expenses.

Should I take a loan if I have an income shock?

Not automatically. Review neutral options such as expense triage, hardship programs, public resources, and trusted advice first.
