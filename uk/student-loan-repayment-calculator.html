How this calculator works

  • Plan 1, 2, 4 or 5: 9% of earnings above that plan's threshold.
  • Postgraduate Loan: 6% of earnings above £21,000 a year, or the corresponding pay-period threshold.
  • Two loan types: the calculator adds the Student Loan and Postgraduate Loan deductions when both are selected.
  • Plan choice: you select the plan yourself. The calculator does not assume a plan solely from your country or location.
  • No balance forecasting: this tool does not estimate interest, payoff dates, cancellation dates, or future salary growth.
