Standard annual, monthly and weekly calculations apply the relevant threshold, calculate the applicable percentage on earnings above it, and round the deduction down to the nearest whole pound.

How this calculator works

  • Plan 1, 2, 4 or 5: 9% of earnings above the applicable threshold.
  • Postgraduate Loan: 6% of earnings above the applicable threshold.
  • Two loan types: the calculator adds Student Loan and Postgraduate Loan deductions when both are selected.
  • Plan choice: you select the plan yourself; the calculator does not assume a plan solely from your country or location.
  • No balance forecasting: this tool does not estimate interest, payoff dates, cancellation dates, or future salary growth.
