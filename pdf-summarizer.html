How to Summarize a PDF with AI?

1. Upload Document

Click on the upload area to select your PDF file. The tool safely extracts the text directly within your browser without uploading the file to a server.

2. Customize Output

Select your desired summary length, output format, and even translate the summary into another language like Urdu or Spanish.

3. Generate Insights

Click the summarize button. Our AI will read the content and instantly provide a clear, actionable summary that you can copy or download.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF files secure?
Yes, absolutely. We use an advanced client-side processing library (PDF.js). This means the text is extracted directly on your device, and your original PDF document is never uploaded or saved to our servers.
Can it summarize large research papers or books?
The AI is highly capable of summarizing multi-page documents, research papers, and corporate reports. It extracts the core meaning and key findings so you don't have to read hours of text.
Can I translate the summary into Urdu or Spanish?
Yes! You can upload a PDF in English and instruct the AI to generate the summary in Urdu, Spanish, French, or several other languages available in the settings.
