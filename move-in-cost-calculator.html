What this estimate includes

The calculator separates first rent, security deposit, application and administration fees, utility setup, moving, furniture or household setup, and other one-time cash you choose to enter. It compares that total with available cash and shows how much would remain or be needed.

Do not assume an unknown cost is zero forever. Ask for the written lease terms and provider requirements, then update the scenario.

Questions people ask

What should I include in move-in costs?

Include the amounts you know for first rent, security deposit, application or administration fees, utility deposits, moving, setup, and other property-specific charges.

Does this calculator know my state security-deposit law?

No. Deposit rules vary by jurisdiction and lease. Confirm the actual terms with the landlord, housing authority, or qualified local source.

Does a positive result mean I can afford the apartment?

No. It compares the cash amounts you enter. Monthly affordability, emergency reserves, debt, and recurring costs still need a separate check.
