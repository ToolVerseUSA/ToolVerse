Frequently asked questions

What is refinance break-even?

It is the approximate month when cumulative payment savings offset refinance costs.

Why can a new 30-year term cost more?

Restarting a longer term can extend interest even when the rate and payment are lower.

Should I refinance based only on payment?

No. Compare total interest, costs, time in the home, and risks—not just payment.
