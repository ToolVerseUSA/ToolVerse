Emergency Funds and Income Shocks: How to Measure Runway

Estimate emergency-fund runway and income-shock deficits while keeping options neutral.

What this decision involves

An emergency fund is a cash-flow buffer, not a guarantee against every shock. Runway depends on liquid savings and essential expenses, while an income shock can create a monthly deficit when income falls below required outflows.

How to use the calculator

The calculator divides liquid savings by essential monthly expenses, compares income with essential expenses plus debt payments, and estimates the savings required for a target number of months.

Open the calculator

Important limitations

If you are under immediate financial pressure, neutral options may include prioritizing essential bills, contacting creditors about hardship programs, reviewing public benefits, and seeking qualified advice. A new loan is not an automatic solution.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as an essential expense?

Expenses needed to maintain basic housing, food, utilities, transportation, insurance, and required obligations are common starting points.

How is runway calculated?

Liquid savings divided by essential monthly expenses.

Should I take a loan if I have an income shock?

Not automatically. Review neutral options such as expense triage, hardship programs, public resources, and trusted advice first.
