Frequently asked questions

How is debt consolidation different from settlement?

Consolidation replaces several debts with one new loan or balance. Settlement seeks creditor agreement to accept less than owed, often with different fees and credit consequences.

Does debt settlement guarantee savings?

No. Creditors may refuse, fees vary, and any modeled result is only a scenario.

Can settlement affect my credit or taxes?

Delinquency may damage credit and canceled debt can have tax consequences. Review official guidance and qualified advice.
