Frequently asked questions

Can refinancing lower my payment but raise total cost?

Yes. Extending the term can reduce the payment while increasing total interest. Compare both measures.

What does negative equity mean?

Negative equity means the payoff balance is higher than the vehicle value used in the comparison.

Does this calculator provide a lender offer?

No. It is a planning estimate, not a lender offer or approval decision.
