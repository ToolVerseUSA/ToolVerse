Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to merge PDF files using ToolVerse?
Yes, absolutely. ToolVerse processes all PDF files locally within your browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring 100% privacy and data security. You can safely merge confidential business and personal documents.
How do I rearrange the order of my PDF files before merging?
Once you upload or drag and drop your PDF files into the workspace, you can simply click, hold, and drag the file thumbnails to reorder them exactly how you want them to appear in the final merged document.
Is there a limit on file size or the number of PDFs I can merge?
Because the processing happens locally on your device, there are no strict artificial limits imposed by our servers. The only limit is your device's available memory (RAM). Most modern devices can easily handle dozens of large PDF files at once.
Can I merge PDF files on my Mac or mobile device?
Yes! Since our Merge PDF tool is fully browser-based, it works flawlessly on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS without requiring any software app installation.
